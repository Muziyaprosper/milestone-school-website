About Prosper

Modern ICT educator, builder, and creative entrepreneur

Prosper Muziya is a Zambian ICT educator, entrepreneur, programmer, and digital creative with a strong passion for technology, education, and innovation. He currently works at Milestone School in Kabwe, Zambia, where he serves as both the Head of the ICT Department and an Administration Assistant.

He teaches ICT from Grades 2 to 7 using practical and blended learning methods while also supporting school operations, digital systems, workflow management, and educational technology integration. His work strengthens ICT Teacher Zambia practice, educational technology Zambia adoption, and day-to-day digital coordination.

Prosper is also the founder of The Future, a growing initiative focused on educational materials, technology services, digital creativity, and innovation. Through this brand, he provides pamphlet production, laptop sales, branding, digital content creation, website design, and social media management.

With a deep interest in Artificial Intelligence and modern digital tools, Prosper aims to simplify technology and make it accessible to learners, businesses, and everyday users.
